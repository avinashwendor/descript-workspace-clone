# Advanced Descript Pro Capabilities Reference

Beyond standard cuts and captions, Descript equipped with Underlord AI provides advanced broadcast-level tools:

---

## 1. AI Vision & Framing Features

### 👁️ AI Eye Contact Correction
- Automatically adjusts the speaker's gaze toward the camera lens in real-time.
- Perfect for creators who look at teleprompters, notes, or off-center monitors during recording.

### 🎭 AI Background Removal / Green Screen (Rotoscoping)
- Isolates subjects without requiring a physical green screen backdrop.
- Allows placing custom dynamic motion backgrounds, branded gradients, screen shares, or game footage behind the speaker.

### 🎥 Multi-Camera & Auto-Speaker Switching
- Automatically identifies multiple speakers from audio channels and switches camera angles to whoever is currently talking.
- Supports side-by-side split screens and picture-in-picture layouts.

### 🔍 Smart Camera Punch-Ins & Pans
- Programmatically adds subtle camera movement (e.g. 1.15x zoom on emphasis words, slow push-ins during storytelling moments).
- Eliminates visual monotony in single-take talking-head videos.

---

## 2. Advanced Audio & Voice Synthesis

### 🎙️ Studio Sound 2.0
- AI-driven room acoustic correction: removes echo, HVAC hum, traffic noise, and microphone pops.
- Matches vocal warmth and compression of a $500 studio microphone setup.

### 🗣️ Overdub / AI Voice Correction
- Fixes mispronounced names, dates, or missing phrases by typing the replacement text directly into the transcript.
- Uses a personalized, ethical voice clone to re-synthesize the corrected words without needing to re-record.

### 🔊 Intelligent Auto-Ducking & Sound Design
- Dynamically reduces background music volume by -18dB whenever speech is detected, smoothly rising back during natural pauses.
- Synchronizes transitional sound effects (whoosh, pop, ding, riser) with on-screen visual cues.

---

## 3. Engagement & Retention Enhancers

### 📊 Dynamic Progress Bars & Timers
- Adds sleek top/bottom animated progress bars to keep viewers watching until the video ends (essential for high short-form completion rates).

### 🌊 Animated Soundwave Visualizers
- Generates dynamic, audio-reactive waveforms and circular audiograms for podcasts, audiobooks, and music clips.

### 🔤 Kinetic Caption Styling
- Word-by-word active highlighting with customizable colors (e.g., vibrant yellow/green highlight, black stroke, modern sans-serif fonts).
- Automatic emoji insertion matching spoken sentiment (🔥, 💡, 🚀).

---

## 4. Repurposing & Multi-Format Workflows

### ✂️ AI Viral Short-Form Clips Generator
- Automatically scans long-form recordings (30m–2h) and extracts the top 3–5 most viral, high-energy standalone short clips (with hooks and titles).

### 📑 Automated Chapter Generation & Summaries
- Creates YouTube-ready timestamped chapter markers and SEO-optimized video descriptions directly from the narrative flow.

### 🌍 Multilingual Subtitles & Translation
- Translates transcripts and captions into 20+ languages for international reach.
